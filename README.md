# HealthScope – Partner Service

The **Partner Service** in HealthScope enables trusted organizations (NGOs, health associations, and institutions) to onboard and manage contributors who publish expert-verified health content on the platform.

## Role of Partners
Partners act as **gatekeepers** for trusted health information within the HealthScope ecosystem. They are responsible for ensuring contributors are properly invited, managed, and their content impact is measurable.

## Capabilities
- **Contributor Management**
  - Invite contributors (single or bulk).
  - Assign roles (basic or super contributor).
- **Content Oversight**
  - Track performance and engagement of articles created by their contributors.
- **Analytics**
  - Download engagement and impact reports for data-driven insights.

## Why It Matters
The Partner Service ensures that HealthScope maintains a **credible, scalable, and locally relevant ecosystem** of verified health information. By empowering organizations to manage their contributor networks, HealthScope builds accountability and strengthens trust across Africa’s digital health landscape.
